# InterLude 0.1.0

The first public release. InterLude plays BBC radio about a minute behind live and mutes the news bulletins, on your Mac or on Chromecast speakers and groups.

## What's in it

- Plays any of 64 BBC stations, national, nations and local, a fixed time behind live (60 seconds by default, adjustable per station).
- Mutes bulletins on music stations, starting at the news theme and ending at the newsreader's sign-off, so the presenter's handover and chat afterwards still play.
- Learns a station's news theme from the last few hours of broadcast the first time you play it.
- Silence during bulletins, or ocean waves, a woodland stream or birdsong if you prefer.
- **Not news** and **News got through** to correct it, and a log of every decision.
- Plays on the Mac, a Chromecast or a Cast group, and you can switch speaker while it plays.

## Before you install

- Needs macOS 26 or later on an Apple silicon Mac.
- Not notarised: the first time, allow it under System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway (steps in the README). Or build it from source.
- Unofficial and not affiliated with the BBC. It relies on stream addresses the BBC doesn't document, which can change; the station list is editable if they do.
- BBC streams may not play outside the UK.
- Detection has been tuned and tested on BBC Radio 6 Music. Other music stations should work but haven't been measured.
