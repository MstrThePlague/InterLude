# InterLude 0.7.0

Bulletin times are now learned per station instead of assumed to be :00 and :30, and streams with no news are never muted.

## What's new

- **Bulletin minutes learned per station.** Every station starts listening at :00 and :30, as before. Once it has a dozen confirmed bulletins, its bulletin minutes follow where they actually started.
  - Starts are counted at the nearest quarter hour, so a bulletin anchored on its theme just before the hour still counts as :00. A quarter hour becomes a bulletin minute when at least 15 % of bulletins, and at least three, started there.
  - A station whose news is only on the hour stops listening at :30, so presenter talk there is no longer at risk of being muted.
  - Bulletins at other times, such as :45, are picked up once **News got through** has marked a few.
  - The counts are halved once they pass 200, so a schedule change is followed.
  - Setting **Bulletin minutes** in Settings fixes them for that station, and learning stops there.
- **Streams with no news are left alone.** Radio 1 Dance, Radio 1 Anthems, Radio 3 Unwind and 6 Music Indie Forever carry no bulletins, so InterLude no longer listens for news on them. Mute news shows as off with "this stream has no news bulletins", and **Not news** and **News got through** are hidden. They play as speech stations always have.

## Changes for people who build or run it

- `stations.json` gains `"news": false` for a stream without bulletins. `Station.mutesNews` (a music station that carries news) replaces checks of `kind == .music` for whether to transcribe, learn the theme and mute. Saved station lists pick up the flag.
- Station profiles gain `slotMinutesByHand`, set when the bulletin minutes are changed in Settings.
- The event log gains a `slotsLearned` event when a station's bulletin minutes change.

## Before you install

- The Mac app needs macOS 26 or later on an Apple silicon Mac.
- A station that has played for a while may already have a dozen bulletins counted, so its minutes can change at its next bulletin. Settings shows the current ones.
- Not notarised: the first time, allow it under System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway (steps in the README). Or build it from source.
- The server image is `ghcr.io/mstrtheplague/interlude-server:0.7.0`. Update with `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`.
- Unofficial and not affiliated with the BBC. It relies on stream addresses the BBC doesn't document, which can change; the station list is editable if they do.
