# InterLude 0.4.0

Adds a **server version**: InterLude now also runs as a web app on a Linux machine, so it plays and mutes the news with no Mac involved. The Mac app gains Earlier programmes on the phone remote.

## What's new

- **InterLude as a server.** Run it with Docker on a Linux machine (x86-64) and open it in any browser, on a phone, tablet or computer.
  - It plays in the browser and can cast to Chromecast speakers and groups itself.
  - The page is the phone remote, grown into the whole app, with two columns on a computer and a Settings sheet.
  - Track, artwork and play/stop show on the phone's lock screen. Add it to the Home Screen to keep it as an app.
  - Setup, network needs and Tailscale are in the README under "Running the server".
- **Open models on the server.** In place of Apple's built-in models, the server uses YAMNet (speech and music) and whisper.cpp (the newsreader's opening and sign-off), both running on its own processor. Nothing is sent to a speech service.
  - On 5¾ hours of 6 Music, the server found the same five bulletins as the Mac app, and the same sign-offs a few seconds later.
- **Jingles carry across.** A news jingle the Mac app has learned can be copied to the server with its station profile.
- **Earlier programmes on the phone remote (Mac).** Switch the phone to Earlier, step through the last 30 days, and tap a programme to play it from its start. The phone shows how far in you are and how much news was skipped.
- **Phone remote over Tailscale (Mac).** If the Mac runs Tailscale, the remote's QR code links to its tailnet name, so the remote also works away from home. The Home Screen icon now shows properly on iOS 27.

## Not yet

- On the server, starting an earlier programme takes up to a minute while it listens for the news at the top of the hour.
- There is no Windows version yet.

## Before you install

- The Mac app needs macOS 26 or later on an Apple silicon Mac.
- Not notarised: the first time, allow it under System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway (steps in the README). Or build it from source.
- The server image is `ghcr.io/mstrtheplague/interlude-server:0.4.0`. It needs Docker and about 1 GB of memory. To cast, the server must reach your speakers and see their multicast DNS (see the README).
- Unofficial and not affiliated with the BBC. It relies on stream addresses the BBC doesn't document, which can change; the station list is editable if they do.
- BBC streams and BBC Sounds episodes may not play outside the UK.
- Detection has been tuned and tested on BBC Radio 6 Music. Other music stations should work but haven't been measured.
