# InterLude 0.2.0

Adds **Earlier**: play a programme from the last 30 days from its start, with the news bulletins cut out.

## What's new

- **Earlier mode.** Switch the panel from Live to Earlier, step back through the last 30 days and pick a programme. It plays from the start, on the Mac or on Cast speakers.
- **The news is skipped, not silenced.** In an earlier programme InterLude leaves each bulletin out and carries straight on, so there's no gap. A short note says how much was skipped.
- **Where programmes come from.** Anything that began in the last 11 hours or so plays from the BBC's live stream. Older programmes play from their BBC Sounds episode.
- **Better at catching bulletins the presenter talks straight into.** In an earlier programme the analysis runs about ten minutes ahead of you, so InterLude also transcribes around every :00 and :30 and starts the cut where the newsreader begins, so the presenter's handover still plays.
- **Progress.** A bar shows how far through the programme you are and where the news was, with a count of bulletins skipped and the time saved.

## Not yet

- There's no seeking within a programme: it plays from the start.
- Bulletins are cut on 6.4 second segment boundaries, so a few seconds either side can go with them.

## Before you install

- Needs macOS 26 or later on an Apple silicon Mac.
- Not notarised: the first time, allow it under System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway (steps in the README). Or build it from source.
- Unofficial and not affiliated with the BBC. It relies on stream addresses the BBC doesn't document, which can change; the station list is editable if they do.
- BBC streams and BBC Sounds episodes may not play outside the UK.
- Detection has been tuned and tested on BBC Radio 6 Music. Other music stations should work but haven't been measured.
