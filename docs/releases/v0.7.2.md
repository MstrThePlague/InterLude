# InterLude 0.7.2

Newsbeat's wording, checked against four hours of Radio 1, 1Xtra, Asian Network and Radio 3.

## What's changed

- **Newsbeat's sign-off ends the mute where it should.** "This is Newsbeat at 10.33" and "… on Newsbeat with Jordan at 12.45" are recognised. A news name on its own no longer counts as a sign-off, since reports say things like "talking to Newsbeat" halfway through. "That's 6 Music News for tonight" still ends 6 Music's bulletins.
- **More ways of hearing "Newsbeat".** Radio 1, 1Xtra and Asian Network also accept "news beat", "newsbeats" and "Newsby", which is how the on-device model often writes it. All three stations carry the same Newsbeat bulletin.
- **"The latest news from …" counts as a handover**, as in "let's get the latest news from Callum Leslie".
- **"This is BBC Radio …" no longer counts as a newsreader's opening.** Presenters say it as a station ident: on Radio 3 at a quarter past, and on Radio 1 straight after the news. 6 Music's bulletins are still found by "BBC News at", and by the theme.
- Spoken times written as "1033" or "1245" are read as times.
- Saved station lists, on the Mac and in the server's `config` folder, now gain news names added to the bundled list, not just names for stations that had none. Names you've added yourself are kept.

On the recordings, Radio 1's half-hour bulletins are now found within 3 seconds of their start, and their sign-offs within a second of their real end. The measurements are in `docs/archive/findings.md`.

## Changes for people who build or run it

- `signoff-check <dir>:slots` transcribes from a minute before to five minutes after every quarter hour of a recording, reporting the opening, handover and sign-off found with the whole transcript. `STATION=<serviceId>` uses that station's news names.

## Still to come

- The 15-minute Newsbeat at 12:45 isn't muted, since :45 isn't a bulletin minute by default and it runs past the longest mute. You can add 45 to Radio 1's bulletin minutes in Settings for now.
- Radio 3 stays `untested`: its presenters' talk near the hour started five false mutes in four hours.

## Before you install

- The Mac app needs macOS 26 or later on an Apple silicon Mac.
- Not notarised: the first time, allow it under System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway (steps in the README). Or build it from source.
- The server image is `ghcr.io/mstrtheplague/interlude-server:0.7.2`. Update with `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`.
- Unofficial and not affiliated with the BBC. It relies on stream addresses the BBC doesn't document, which can change; the station list is editable if they do.
