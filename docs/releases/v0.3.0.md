# InterLude 0.3.0

Adds a **phone remote**: control InterLude from a phone on the same Wi-Fi, with nothing to install.

## What's new

- **Phone remote.** Turn it on in Settings › Phone remote and scan the QR code with your phone's camera. The remote opens in the phone's browser.
- **What it does.** Play and stop, volume, station and speaker. It shows what's on and whether a bulletin is muted, with the **Not news** and **News got through** buttons.
- **Keep it on your home screen.** On an iPhone, tap Share › Add to Home Screen. It opens full screen with the InterLude icon.
- **Private link.** The remote's address carries a long random code, so others on your network can't guess it. **Reset link** makes a new one, and the old link stops working.
- **Plays live.** Pressing Play or picking a station on the phone plays it live. If the Mac was on Earlier, it switches to Live.

## Not yet

- Earlier programmes can't be chosen from the phone yet.

## Before you install

- Needs macOS 26 or later on an Apple silicon Mac.
- Not notarised: the first time, allow it under System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway (steps in the README). Or build it from source.
- If the Mac's firewall is on, macOS asks once whether InterLude may accept incoming connections. Allow it for the phone remote to work.
- The Mac has to be awake with InterLude open for the remote to reach it.
- Unofficial and not affiliated with the BBC. It relies on stream addresses the BBC doesn't document, which can change; the station list is editable if they do.
- BBC streams and BBC Sounds episodes may not play outside the UK.
- Detection has been tuned and tested on BBC Radio 6 Music. Other music stations should work but haven't been measured.
